# MathXY

## Overview

A program that helps students with math, specifically with algebra and geometry. It includes an AI that helps you understand said topics and gives you problems for you to solve. **MathXY** allows you to input a topic and it will swiftly give you the explanation and the overview of said topic.

This program helps is focused on Pisay students who struggle on math especially grade 8 students with Math a, b (a.k.a Algebra, and Geometry). You can save your topics in a built in file system so you wont lose track of your progress. Since it is powered by Ai, it can make custom shortcuts or quick solutions for long problems with a minimal margin of error. 

## Features

A free Python-based AI that takes a plain
algebra or geometry question and shows every step. No sign-up,
no subscription.

- Solve algebra problems (equations, factoring, simplification)
- Solve geometry problems (area, distance, angles, intersections)
- Show every step, not just the final answer
- Keep it free and simple, no sign-up required

- A simple interface (CLI or web) instead of raw terminal input
- Step-by-step explanations for factoring and solving equations
- Drawing geometry shapes so you can *see* the triangle/line
- Saving past questions so you can review them later
- Supporting more problem types (quadratics, circles, etc.)

# Project Proposal

## Project Title: MathXY

## Problem Statement:

Students stuck on algebra or geometry homework need someone to
walk them through it, not just hand them the answer.

- **Algebra** – "Solve for x: 2x + 5 = 13", "Factor x² − 9"
- **Geometry** – "Area of triangle (0,0), (4,0), (2,3)?",
  "Are these lines parallel?"

The problem is, most tools just spit out a number.  You still
don't know *why* x = 4. Geometry is worse because a calculator
can't even try.

Existing options either give bare answers, hallucinate steps,
or want your email and a credit card.

## Project Objectives:

Build a free, accessible learning tool that helps students overcome difficulties in algebra and geometry by providing clear, step-by-step explanations and visual diagrams for visual learners rather than just final answers that students get confused with.

## Features:

What we built: A free Python-based AI that takes a plain
algebra or geometry question and shows every step. No sign-up,
no subscription.

- Solve algebra problems (equations, factoring, simplification)
- Solve geometry problems (area, distance, angles, intersections)
- Show every step, not just the final answer
- Keep it free and simple, no sign-up required

- A simple interface (CLI or web) instead of raw terminal input
- Step-by-step explanations for factoring and solving equations
- Drawing geometry shapes so you can *see* the triangle/line
- Saving past questions so you can review them later
- Supporting more problem types (quadratics, circles, etc.)

## Inputs:

Text typed into the terminal (or web form once that's done).
It should handle things like "solve 2x + 5 = 13",
"factor x^2 - 9", "area of triangle with points
(0,0), (4,0), (2,3)", or "are the lines y = 2x + 1
and y = 2x - 3 parallel?"

## Outputs:

For each question, it prints the steps, then the answer.
For geometry, it also draws the shape using matplotlib
and shows the coordinates of any intersection points.
